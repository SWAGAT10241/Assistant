# Security Policy

## Security Policy

This document outlines the security policies for the Assistant repository. It is essential to adhere to this policy to help maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our software.

### Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a vulnerability in this project, please report it to us as soon as possible. 

1. **Email**: Please reach out to security@swagat.com.
2. **Responsibility**: After receiving your report, we will assess it and respond within 48 hours.
3. **Disclosure**: We will keep your report confidential and will not disclose any information without your consent until we have resolved the issue.

### Security Guidelines
- Please ensure your local environment is secure.
- Keep your dependencies updated.
- Regularly check for security advisories related to packages in use.

Thank you for helping us keep our software secure!
